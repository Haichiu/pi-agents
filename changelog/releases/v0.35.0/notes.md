## 🚀 Features

### Mouse-wheel scrolling in the attached view

The mouse wheel now scrolls an attached agent's conversation in fullscreen mode, the default. Point at the attached view and scroll as you would in Pi's own transcript; Shift+↑ ↓ and Shift+PgUp/PgDn keep working.

In regular mode, the terminal keeps handling the mouse wheel, so use the keyboard to scroll the attached view there.

*By @mavam in #103.*

## 🔧 Changes

### Separator above the attached view

A muted horizontal line now separates an attached agent's conversation from Pi's view above it, matching the editor's bottom border. The agent's name and status stay on the editor's top border, and the attached view keeps its height, so only the conversation gives up a line.

*By @mavam in #103.*
